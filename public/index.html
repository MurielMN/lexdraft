Common questions

Are these documents legally valid?

A document's validity depends on its content and on proper signing, witnessing and notarization under your state's law, not on how it was drafted. Have a licensed attorney review anything important.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can generate, copy and print documents without signing up.

Is my information stored?

The generator runs in your browser. We do not save what you type into the form.

Is LexDraftVault a law firm?

No. We provide self-help tools, not legal advice. See our disclaimer.
